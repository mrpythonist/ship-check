# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in ShipCheck a harassment-free experience
for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender
identity and expression, level of experience, nationality, personal
appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

- Be kind, direct, and honest. Critique the code, not the person.
- Assume good faith. Ask before assuming intent.
- Respect differing viewpoints and experiences.

## Unacceptable behaviour

Harassment, intimidation, insulting or derogatory comments, unwelcome
sexual attention, and publishing others' private information.

## Enforcement

Report violations to the maintainers via a GitHub issue marked private
or by email to the maintainer listed on the repository. Maintainers will
review and respond, and may remove contributions or ban participants who
violate this code.
